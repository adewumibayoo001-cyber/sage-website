Garlic Butter Chicken with Sautéed Greens

⏱ 18 mins • ~440 kcal • 45g Protein

Zero Waste

"Sear seasoned chicken breast in golden garlic butter, then fold in fresh spinach for the last 2 minutes until tender. High protein, ready in under 20 minutes."

Cooking Instructions:

  1. Cut chicken breast into bite-sized tenders; season with salt, pepper, and garlic.
  2. Melt butter in a medium skillet; cook chicken 5-6 mins until juices run clear.
  3. Toss spinach into the pan drippings for 90 seconds until wilted. Serve hot!
