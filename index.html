Garlic-Butter Seared Salmon with Spinach

⏱ 18 mins • 480 kcal • 42g Protein

100% Pantry Match

"You have fresh salmon and spinach recorded as expiring soon. This skillet meal crisps the salmon skin in garlic butter and wilts the spinach in the flavorful pan drippings."

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Pat salmon dry, season with coarse salt, pepper, and minced garlic.
  2. Heat skillet with butter over medium-high heat. Sear salmon 4 mins per side.
  3. Add fresh spinach to pan edges, toss for 90 seconds until bright and tender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fridzo and Miso before you download.

Is Fridzo really free to use?

Yes! Fridzo's core pantry management across your fridge, pantry, and freezer, expiry alerts, weekly meal planner, barcode scanner, and shopping lists are completely free with no trial limit. Fridzo Pro unlocks unlimited Miso AI Chef conversations, receipt photo scanning, and macro analytics.

What is Miso and how does it create recipes?

Miso is your in-app AI chef. Instead of giving you generic internet recipes that require an expensive supermarket run, Miso reads what is currently in your fridge and pantry and constructs delicious recipes prioritizing items approaching expiry.

How does receipt scanning work?

Simply snap a photo of any printed grocery receipt using the in-app camera. Fridzo's OCR engine recognizes item names, estimates shelf-lives, and populates your pantry in seconds so you never have to type items manually.

Is Fridzo available on iPhone / iOS?

Fridzo is currently available for Android on the Google Play Store. An iOS version is actively in development. You can join the iOS waitlist at the top of the page to be notified the moment TestFlight opens!

Can I cancel Fridzo Pro anytime?

Yes. All subscriptions are managed safely through your Google Play account with zero hidden fees. You can cancel with one click in the Play Store at any time.
